# ADR-0003 — Paid Lead Enrichment
Status: Approved — CTO decision
Date: 2026-09-24
Scope: Lead enrichment, cleanup, additional lead discovery, UI, billing

## 1. Decision context

Lead enrichment is a separate downstream research action after SERP
collection/research. It must improve existing leads while also allowing the
crawl/research process to discover additional qualifying people.

Enrichment must never replace the existing SERP extraction/qualification
pipeline. Existing Lead acceptance, validation, provenance, persistence, and
deduplication rules remain authoritative.

## 2. Paid Enrichment

Scrappee exposes one explicit enrichment mode: **Paid Enrichment**.

There is no Free Enrichment mode. No separate unpaid enrichment path is
supported by this ADR.

The mode uses the same final validation, qualification, deduplication,
persistence, evidence, and export boundaries as the core pipeline.

## 3. Paid Enrichment Architecture

Paid Enrichment combines:

- Firecrawl Search for enrichment-time source discovery
- Firecrawl page/content retrieval for relevant evidence
- GPT-OSS-20B for contextual research, extraction, and correction
- Scrappee's existing validation, qualification, deduplication, and persistence
  pipeline

Flow:

Current Scrap selected working leads
→ structured field-aware query planning
→ Firecrawl Search
→ source-policy filtering and relevance ranking
→ targeted Firecrawl page/content retrieval
→ evidence reduction
→ GPT-OSS-20B contextual extraction/research
→ candidate generation/correction
→ Pydantic validation
→ qualification
→ lead deduplication
→ persist enriched existing leads and genuinely new qualifying leads

The existing SERP/search acquisition layer is unchanged. Scrapy remains the
normal broad crawl engine; Firecrawl is the enrichment engine.

### 3.1 Structured query and source policy

Paid Enrichment queries MUST be generated by a structured, field-aware query
planner rather than by unconstrained field concatenation. The planner should
use the Lead's available identity, role, company, location, and contact fields
to produce bounded search intents such as identity confirmation, professional
identity/company discovery, and contact/source discovery.

Email-aware source policy:

- If the selected Lead already has an email, LinkedIn is an allowed enrichment
  source and may provide identity, role, company, or corroborating evidence.
- If the selected Lead has no email, LinkedIn MUST NOT be treated as sufficient
  enrichment evidence for email recovery. Search must prioritize independent,
  non-LinkedIn sources capable of supplying or validating the missing email.
- When email is missing, LinkedIn may still support identity or role
  confirmation, but it cannot by itself satisfy the missing-email enrichment
  objective.
- The original source domain remains provenance and is excluded from
  independent-source enrichment evidence under the cross-source rule below.

The query planner, source filtering, and evidence policy MUST be observable in
job telemetry so query intent and source decisions can be audited.

GPT-OSS-20B output is untrusted research/extraction input and never bypasses
Pydantic validation, qualification, or deduplication.

## 5. Existing versus newly discovered leads

Enrichment has two valid outputs:

- Existing lead: merge supported corrections and missing fields into the
  existing identity.
- New lead: persist a newly discovered person when the normal Lead acceptance
  contract is satisfied.

Both paths use the same authoritative persistence and deduplication path.
No separate enrichment-only Lead schema is permitted.
## 6. Cleanup and correction

Enrichment may correct extraction artifacts, including misplaced page titles,
document headings, category labels, URL fragments, and invalid supporting
fields.

Existing valid person-specific data must not be overwritten by weaker or
non-person-specific evidence.

Every resulting Lead must pass the normal Pydantic validation and qualification
rules before it is retained/exported.

## 7. UI

The Lead Workstation contains the **PAID ENRICHMENT** action alongside the
working-lead controls.

Paid Enrichment is selection-driven: only explicitly selected eligible working
Leads are submitted to the enrichment job.

The UI must show the applicable configured price before confirmation, together
with the selected Lead count and calculated amount. The job must expose running
status, progress, discovered leads, enrichment evidence, and terminal result.
## 8. Job model

Paid Enrichment is a normal persistent Job associated with the Scrap.

The job must retain:

- enrichment mode
- selected/seed lead count
- Firecrawl searches and sources
- pages/content collected
- evidence count
- GPT-OSS-20B calls and usage telemetry when available
- existing leads enriched
- new lead candidates
- final qualified leads
- deduplication results
- cost telemetry
- terminal status and diagnostics

## 9. Billing

Paid Enrichment is billable. The price is administrator-configurable,
persistent, auditable, and charged atomically/idempotently before execution.

The Paid Enrichment customer price is **$0.001 per selected Lead**. Billing uses the wallet's cent granularity, so charges are rounded up to the nearest cent with a minimum charge of $0.01 per execution. 10,000 selected Leads therefore cost $10.00.

Infrastructure/provider usage cost is tracked separately from the customer
price. Cost optimization and provider benchmarking are engineering concerns;
they do not change the single Paid Enrichment product mode.
## 10. Safety and controls

Paid Enrichment remains bounded by Scrap-level lead selection, provider,
request, duration, retry, and evidence limits.

Firecrawl Search is the approved enrichment search capability. Scrappee must
not implement CAPTCHA solving, credential theft, session theft, or search-engine
anti-bot bypass.

GPT-OSS-20B output is untrusted extraction/research input and must pass the same
validation and qualification boundary as all other extracted candidates.

## 11. Deduplication

Enrichment must never bypass the existing Lead deduplication system.

If a newly discovered person matches an existing Lead, the system merges the
supported evidence/fields rather than creating an uncontrolled duplicate.

URL occurrence/provenance and evidence remain independently retained.
## 12. Relationship to existing architecture

This ADR extends the V1.3 enrichment definition. The existing pipeline remains:

SERP acquisition
→ URL validation/deduplication
→ Scrapy collection
→ evidence
→ extraction
→ validation
→ qualification
→ lead deduplication/persistence
→ Paid Enrichment when explicitly requested

Paid Enrichment is the only enrichment mode. It can produce both corrections
to existing leads and genuinely new qualifying leads.

The existing V1.3 Lead acceptance contract remains authoritative.

## 13. Engineering benchmark

Provider benchmarking is an engineering measurement activity, not a second product mode. The approved baseline is the optimized Firecrawl + GPT-OSS-20B path.

Benchmark candidates:

1. optimized Firecrawl + GPT-OSS-20B baseline
2. cheaper LLM alternative, subject to provider availability and current pricing
3. deterministic extraction with LLM fallback

Measure on the same frozen enrichment evidence set:

- cost per seed lead
- valid-field recovery
- attribution correctness
- false-positive/new-lead rate
- latency
- provider usage/tokens

No benchmark may modify production qualification, persistence, billing, or the stable pipeline. External provider calls are billable and require configured provider credentials; local harness validation must remain non-billable.

## 14. CTO decisions

The CTO has approved Paid Enrichment as the sole enrichment mode.

Commercial configuration approved by the CTO:

1. Paid Enrichment customer unit price: $0.001 per selected Lead.
2. Billing granularity: wallet cents; round up with a $0.01 minimum execution charge.

The provider architecture is fixed for the current implementation:
Firecrawl Search + Firecrawl content retrieval + GPT-OSS-20B. Provider pricing and
actual usage cost remain engineering telemetry inputs and may be benchmarked
without creating a second product mode.

### Cross-source enrichment identity rule
Paid Enrichment is explicitly a cross-source research operation. The selected Lead's `source_url` is provenance only and MUST NOT be used as an identity requirement, exclusion rule, or same-person match condition during enrichment. Firecrawl Search/Scrape is expected to discover other websites and independent evidence about the person.

Enrichment identity resolution MUST use person attributes/evidence such as normalized email, phone, name+company, or name+geography where supported. A different source URL is not evidence that the candidate is a different person. The original source URL MUST be preserved as provenance, while newly discovered source URLs/evidence are attached to the Lead.

The implementation MUST NOT fall back to source-URL equality merely because an enrichment source differs.
Independent-source requirement: Paid Enrichment MUST prioritize sources outside the selected Lead's original source domain. The original source URL is provenance, not an enrichment target. Same-domain results alone do not constitute cross-source enrichment. If no independent external source can be discovered/retrieved, the system must not claim a Lead was enriched or manufacture a correction/new Lead from the original source metadata alone.


Paid Enrichment is charged only when an enrichment provider is actually called. The initial wallet debit may reserve the execution charge, but if the job terminates before any Firecrawl/GPT enrichment provider call occurs, the reserved amount is credited back automatically. Once an enrichment provider call has occurred, the charge is final: cancellation, provider failure, partial completion, or later job failure does not generate a refund or credit.
